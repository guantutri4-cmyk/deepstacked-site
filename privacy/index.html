Privacy Policy for Deepstacked

Effective date: 17 November 2026
Publisher: Deepstacked
Contact: support@deepstacked.app

Deepstacked is a free, offline poker tournament study course.

What we collect: nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party SDKs. It does not connect to a server and does not send any data off your device.

What is stored on your device: your course progress (completed lessons, XP, streak, badges, placement result), your answers and per-topic accuracy, your review schedule and your settings (for example sound, haptics and the daily reminder time). This data stays in the app's local storage on your device. You can reset course progress and drill stats in the app, and clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app erases everything.

Backup file: the app can export your progress to a file (or as text you can copy) and import it again. You start both actions yourself, and where the file is saved or loaded from is your choice. The app does not upload it.

Notifications: if you allow notifications, the app schedules local notifications on your device: an optional daily reminder at the time you pick and a streak nudge. The app asks for permission only after you finish your first lesson, or when you turn the reminder on in Settings. They are generated on the device itself, with no push service and no server, and nothing about them is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the app or in your device settings.

Haptics: the app uses your device's vibration motor for tap feedback while you answer. This needs no personal data and can be switched off in the app.

Children: the app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes: if this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact: support@deepstacked.app

Study tool only. No real-money gambling. Not affiliated with any poker site.
